About me

I'm Govind, a content creator and the founder of RichInd. My work is built around one idea: a good Reel is short, honest, and leaves the viewer a little better than it found them.

Most of my stories are about values: choosing truth when lying seems easier, kindness when the world gets harsh, and humility in service. Devotion, to me, means letting your character and actions speak.

Follow the path of truth. Serve with humility. Live with courage.
